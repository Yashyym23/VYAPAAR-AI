The problem

Your business runs on memory, notebooks and WhatsApp

Most small business owners track sales, udhaar and stock using a mix of notebooks, memory, WhatsApp chats and spreadsheets — or accounting software that's built for accountants, not shopkeepers.

The solution

A conversational assistant that runs alongside you

VyapaarAI listens, understands what you mean, shows you exactly what it understood, and only records it once you confirm. It remembers how your business runs and gets more useful the longer you use it.

How it works

Speak → Understand → Confirm → Record → Analyze → Recommend → Act

The AI is an assistant, not the owner — your business, your final call, every time.

1

Speak

Say it the way you naturally would — English, Hindi, Marathi or a mix.

2

Understand

VyapaarAI detects the language and extracts what you mean — amounts, names, products.

3

Confirm

It shows you exactly what it understood before touching your records.

4

Record

Only after your confirmation does it save the transaction.

5

Analyze

Patterns in sales, stock and payments build up over time into business memory.

6

Recommend

Useful, explained suggestions — reorders, follow-ups, price changes.

7

Act

Send a WhatsApp reminder, prepare a supplier order — you approve every action.
